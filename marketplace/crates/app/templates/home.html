How it works

  • Browse the catalogue — no account needed to look.
  • Register with a username and passphrase. There is no email and no password reset — save the recovery code you are shown.
  • Order from a seller. You pay the seller directly in Monero, wallet to wallet, to a one-time subaddress the seller signed in advance. The order page explains every step, and the payment page shows you how to verify the address in your own wallet before you pay.
  • Messages are end-to-end encrypted on your own device. The server stores ciphertext only and can never read them.
  • Reputation is built from minimized trade facts, shown raw next to any score — including the ways it can be gamed. Read the limitations before trusting it.

Before you trade

Payments here are direct and final: there is no escrow and the marketplace cannot refund you or recover a payment. Read payment risks and the security guide, verify addresses and fingerprints yourself, and start small with any new seller.
