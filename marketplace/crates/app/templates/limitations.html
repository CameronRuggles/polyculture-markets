Limitations

This marketplace is built to give strong guarantees about payment custody and message content, and weak ones about metadata. Those are different properties; this page never blurs them.

What this system does protect

  • Funds never pass through the marketplace. Buyers pay sellers directly, wallet to wallet. A compromised server cannot steal money it never holds.
  • Message content. Messages are encrypted on your device; the database holds ciphertext only. There is no plaintext, no shipping details, and no wallet keys or seeds stored here — ever.
  • Payment addresses. Sellers pre-sign their payment addresses with their own wallet. Your wallet can verify the signature before you pay, so a compromised server cannot silently swap in its own address — if you verify.
  • Accounts. Passphrases are hashed with Argon2id; sessions rotate; every form carries a CSRF token.

What it does not protect against

  • A seller who takes payment and does not deliver. There is no escrow, so every order carries this risk. Reputation and disputes inform you; they cannot make you whole.
  • Compromised devices. If your computer or wallet is compromised, nothing here helps.
  • Traffic correlation and metadata. The operator always knows which accounts interact, which listings they order, and when. Timing and sizes are visible to network observers; use Tor.
  • A malicious server on your first visit, before you have pinned anyone's key. Verify fingerprints against copies obtained elsewhere.
  • Review farming. Weighted reputation raises the cost of sock-puppet farming; it does not prevent it. The raw facts are shown so you can judge for yourself.
  • Skipped verification. The verification steps only protect people who do them.

Payments are attested, not verified

This instance does not verify payments on-chain and runs no wallet. An order shows "seller confirmed payment" because the seller said so after seeing funds in their own wallet. Nothing on this site can prove a payment happened.
