Proof of payment

Strongest protection: submit your transaction proof. After you pay, create the proof in your wallet and paste it here. Submitting it does not mark the order paid — the seller confirms payment from their own wallet, and the order reads paid only then. What a proof does: it stops the order expiring, it means the seller cannot close the order over your payment, and it is what a moderator checks if you dispute. The seller cannot see it. You can submit or revise it at any point while the order is open — including later, if a problem appears.

  • Feather: History → right-click the transaction → Create Tx Proof → choose “Prove payment to an address” → Get formatted proof.
  • Monero GUI: History → click the “P” button on the transaction.
  • Command line: run get_tx_proof in monero-wallet-cli.
No proof? Confirm payment in words alone (weaker protection)

If your wallet cannot create a proof, you can still record that you paid. This is your word alone — weaker protection than a transaction proof: it opens your dispute path, but it does not mark the order paid, does not stop the order expiring, and a seller declaration of non-receipt can close the order over it (you could then dispute the declaration).
